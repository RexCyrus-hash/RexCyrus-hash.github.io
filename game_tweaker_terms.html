⚡ 1. System Optimization

The app applies debug configurations and performance tweaks designed to enhance touch response, UI fluidity, and overall gaming stability.

🛡️ 2. Safe Tuning & System Limits

Game Tweaker operates within safe system boundaries without modifying core hardware parameters beyond Android specifications.
