Game Tweaker

Terms Information

Game Tweaker focuses on optimizing your device through standard system configurations.

1. OPTIMIZATION

The app applies debug configurations designed to enhance touch response, UI fluidity, and overall gaming stability.

2. SAFE TUNING

The tweaks used are standard Android system properties. These are non-permanent and are meant for performance fine-tuning.

3. PERFORMANCE VARIANCE

Because every device is unique, performance gains may vary. We encourage users to experiment with different settings to find what works best for their hardware.

4. PRIVACY & ADS

Your privacy is important. This app collects limited personal information, such as your Gmail address and device IDs, for essential VIP authorization and security. To keep this tool accessible, we also display third-party ads which may use non-personal device identifiers. For more details, please refer to our full Privacy Policy.
