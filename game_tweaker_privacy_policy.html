Game Tweaker Privacy Policy

Game Tweaker ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information within our mobile application.

🔐 VIP Authorization and Security (Device Binding)

To provide Premium/VIP access and prevent unauthorized account sharing, we collect and link your Gmail address with your device's unique identifiers (Android ID and telephony-related identifiers).

  • Purpose: This binding ensures that your VIP access is restricted to authorized devices. Gmail is used solely for account identification and VIP access validation.
  • Storage: This data is securely stored using Google Firebase, a cloud database service provided by Google.
  • No Password Collection: We do not collect or store any passwords or sensitive login credentials.

🔑 VIP Activation & Gift Code System

The app supports an optional access code system for VIP activation used for promotional gifts, tester access, and managing premium rewards.

  • Validation & Limits: Access codes are securely validated through our Firebase database. Codes may have a specific usage limit (number of devices allowed to claim) as determined by the developer.
  • Access Duration & Expiration: Activated VIP status or promotional codes are subject to a specific time duration (number of days) defined by the developer at the time of issuance.
  • Automatic Revocation: To ensure the integrity of the promotion, premium access is programmed to automatically expire and be revoked by the system once the specified timeframe has elapsed.
  • Device Binding: Once successfully claimed, the premium access is securely linked to that specific device identifier to ensure fair distribution of rewards and prevent unauthorized account sharing.
  • Management: The developer reserves the right to manage or revoke codes at any time to prevent fraud or abuse.

📱 Data Collection (General)

Aside from the Gmail address and device identifiers used for VIP access, we do not collect or share personal information. However, third-party services (such as Google AdMob or Firebase) may collect data such as Advertising IDs or basic device info for security and ad delivery.

⚙️ System Optimization and Management (Shizuku API)

To provide advanced gaming optimizations and app management features, Game Tweaker utilizes the Shizuku API.

  • Functionality: We use Shizuku to apply standard Android system properties (tweaks) designed to improve UI responsiveness and gaming stability.
  • No Data Exfiltration: The Shizuku interface is used strictly for executing local system commands on your device. We do not collect, log, or upload any information regarding your system configurations to our servers.
  • User Control: Shizuku access is entirely optional and requires explicit user permission.

🛠️ Feature Usage and Customization (With ADB Shell Protection)

Game Tweaker is designed to provide advanced performance tools.

  • User-Executed Shell Commands (ADB): The user is solely responsible for any scripts or commands they choose to execute through the manual ADB interface. We do not guarantee the safety of user-inputted commands.
  • Liability: While we strive to provide a safe environment, we are not liable for system instability or performance issues resulting from the user's manual input of commands or modification of critical system components.
